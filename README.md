## Hola there!

I’m Kamran Mir Hazar. I work as an educator and write across technology, culture, and identity, with a background in information systems and IT education.

My main interest is how digital systems shape identity, memory, and participation for people and communities that exist outside state‑centric structures. I focus on fundamentals, theory, and framework development, supported by small‑scale technical exploration rather than production systems.

### Areas of work
- Digital identity and self‑sovereign identity concepts  
- Human‑centred and privacy‑preserving system design  
- Digital governance and legitimacy beyond the nation‑state  
- Education‑first approaches to digital technologies  
- The relationship between culture, narrative, and technical systems  

### Ongoing work
- Digital Hazaristan: a Norwegian nonprofit initiative focused on research and framework development around digital identity, governance, and dignity for stateless and marginalized communities  
- Hazaristan Array: a long‑term writing project exploring identity, memory, and continuity from cultural and posthuman perspectives  
- A personal research lab for studying and assessing technologies such as Hyperledger, Go, and containerized environments as analytical tools  

### How I work
I prioritize conceptual clarity before implementation, governance and ethics before infrastructure, and learning through careful experimentation and documentation.
